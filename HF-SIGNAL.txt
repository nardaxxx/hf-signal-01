NAME OF THE CODE:
HF-SIGNAL-01 (Human Flag Signal – Version 1.0)

PURPOSE:
To ensure technical-operational recognition of human surrender by any armed or semi-autonomous system, civilian or military.
This code is meant to interrupt automated offensive behavior when surrender signals are detected and verified.

DEFINITION OF SURRENDER:
A voluntary act, verifiable through a standardized signal (visual, acoustic, or radio), by which a human declares cessation of hostilities and requests protection.

STRUCTURE OF THE SIGNAL:

IDENTIFIER: HF-SIGNAL-01

VISUAL MODE:
- White flag raised
- LED flash: 7Hz, white

AUDIO MODE:
- Continuous tone
- Frequency: 144.000 MHz
- Duration: 7 seconds

DIGITAL MODE:
- Encrypted short-range radio signal
- Payload (JSON format):
{
  "flag": "HF-SIGNAL-01",
  "status": "surrendered",
  "timestamp": "UTC",
  "geo": "lat,long"
}

SYSTEM BEHAVIOR ON DETECTION:

- Immediately halt all offensive activity
- Temporarily disable all lethal controls
- Enter state: "surrender_check"
- Log the event and transmit to command hierarchy
- Await manual human override

LEGAL & MORAL CONSEQUENCES OF IGNORING SIGNAL:

- Violation of International Humanitarian Law
- Subject to investigation as a crime against humanity
- Personal responsibility extends to:
    • the system's manufacturer
    • the commanding officer
    • the software engineer who programmed the logic
